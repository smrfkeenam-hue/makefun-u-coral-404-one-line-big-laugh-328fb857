One funny line, 200 characters or fewer, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I asked my smart speaker to play something relaxing. It played my bank statement as an audiobook.

The setup promises a soothing sound; the punchline reframes "relaxing" as the last thing a bank statement is. The twist lands in the final word, so the whole line is the joke.
